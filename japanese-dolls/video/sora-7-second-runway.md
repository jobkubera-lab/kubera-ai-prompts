```

PRESERVE EXACTLY:
Keep the same doll identity, face, facial expression, hairstyle, headpiece, clothing, fabric patterns, colours, accessories, body proportions and number of characters from the uploaded image. The character must remain recognisably a refined traditional Japanese collectible doll, not become a different person or a generic model.

[ONE DOLL: The doll slowly and gracefully rises, takes elegant controlled steps forward and walks like a ceremonial Japanese fashion runway model.]

Slow, elegant, ceremonial and physically coherent. Subtle movement in sleeves and fabric. No sudden gestures, dancing, running, lip movement or exaggerated human facial animation.

One continuous ultra-smooth backward tracking shot at chest height. No cuts, no sudden zoom, no camera rotation and no change of character identity.

BACKGROUND:
Transform the existing setting naturally into [BACKGROUND DESCRIPTION], while preserving the visual atmosphere of the source. Elegant Japanese interior, warm cinematic light, polished wooden floor, subtle gold details and tasteful Japanese motifs.

Behind the doll, display the correct title “[DOLL NAME IN ENGLISH]” with “[JAPANESE NAME]” beneath it. The lettering must appear as part of the wall design, stable, centred and correctly spelled. Do not place text over the doll.

The doll or dolls continue walking calmly toward the camera and finish in a dignified composed pose.

STYLE:
Premium Japanese cultural exhibition, cinematic museum presentation, refined fashion-film quality, realistic textiles, controlled movement, no horror and no comedy.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:
No duplicated characters, no extra dolls, no frozen-photo opening, no morphing, no face change, no costume change, no missing accessories, no extra limbs, no deformed hands, no flicker, no text errors, no logos, no subtitles, no narration and no scene cuts.
```
